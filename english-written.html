Admission Standard Paragraph Writing

Paragraph 1: Climate Change and Bangladesh
Bangladesh is one of the worst victims of global climate change. Due to its geographical location, the country frequently faces natural disasters like floods, cyclones, and river erosion. Raising global awareness and eco-friendly measures are essential to mitigate this environmental crisis.
Paragraph 2: Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Artificial Intelligence has revolutionized modern technology. It refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines. While it increases efficiency and automates complex tasks, ethical concerns and job displacement remain major challenges for society.

Written Sentence Corrections

• Incorrect: He told that he will come.
➔ Correct: He said that he would come.

• Incorrect: Bread and butter are my favorite breakfast.
➔ Correct: Bread and butter is my favorite breakfast.

• Incorrect: Open page 50.
➔ Correct: Open at page 50 (or Open page 50 without 'at' depending on contexts, but 'Open at page 50' is standard).

Tips for English Written Exam

  • খাতার মার্জিন পরিষ্কার রাখুন এবং কাটাকাটি এড়িয়ে চলুন।
  • অনুবাদ করার সময় আক্ষরিক অনুবাদের চেয়ে ভাবানুবাদ (Sense translation) বেশি গুরুত্ব দিন।
  • বানান ও টেম্পলেট নির্ভুল রাখতে ছোট ও গোছানো বাক্য ব্যবহার করুন।
