HSC Textual English & Admission Vocabulary

ইউনিট নির্বাচন করুন

Unit 1: People or Institutions Making History

  • Key Topics: 7th March Speech of Bangabandhu, Nelson Mandela (from apartheid fighter to President), Valentina Tereshkova & Kalpana Chawla.
  • Admission Focus: Bangabandhu's speech was included in the Memory of the World International Register by UNESCO as a World Documentary Heritage on 30 October 2017.
  • Important Grammar Context: Conditional sentences, Passive voice in historical speeches, Prepositional phrases.

Textual Vocabulary (Synonyms & Antonyms)

Word Meaning Synonym Antonym
Apartheid বর্ণবাদ Racial Segregation, Discrimination Integration, Equality
Emancipation মুক্তি / স্বাধীনতা Liberation, Freedom Bondage, Slavery
Manifesto ইশতেহার Declaration, Policy statement Concealment
Shackle শৃঙ্খল / শিকল Chain, Restraint Liberate, Unbind

Unit 2: Dreams

  • Key Topics: What is a Dream? (Psychological overview), Poems on Dreams by D.H. Lawrence & Langston Hughes.
  • Admission Focus: D.H. Lawrence talks about 'Dreams of the day' vs 'Dreams of the night'. Langston Hughes emphasizes that "If dreams die, life is a broken-winged bird that cannot fly."

Unit 3: Lifestyle & Food Adulteration

  • Key Topics: 'The Luncheon' by W. Somerset Maugham, Food Adulteration, Fitness & Nutrition.
  • Admission Focus: 'The Luncheon' is a famous satirical short story. Notice the ironic tone and the expensive dishes ordered by the woman (Foyot's restaurant, Caviar, Salmon, Champagne, Asparagus, Peach).

Unit 4: Human Relationships & Etiquette

  • Key Topics: Etiquette and Manners, Family Relations, Warmth of Love.
  • Admission Focus: Difference between 'Etiquette' (rules of correct behavior in society) and 'Manners' (the behavior that is considered polite in a particular society or culture).

Unit 5: Youthful Experiences & Adolescence

  • Key Topics: Stages of life, Storm and Stress of Adolescence, Jerry from 'Mother in Mannville' by Marjorie Kinnan Rawlings.
  • Admission Focus: Jerry's character traits (Integrity, Duty, Independence). Note the usage of the phrase "Substrate of integrity".

Unit 6: Environment & Nature

  • Key Topics: Water, Water Everywhere (The Rime of the Ancient Mariner by S.T. Coleridge), Hakaluki Haor, Kuakata (Daughter of the Sea).
  • Admission Focus: Kuakata allows a visitor to watch both the Sunrise and the Sunset from the beach. Coleridge's famous quote: "Water, water, everywhere, / Nor any drop to drink."
